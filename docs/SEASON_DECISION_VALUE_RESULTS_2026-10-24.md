## Fixed-roster progression stage

All three selections were frozen on 456 panels before new evaluator access.
The primary four saved H6 paths gained +9, +4, −3 and +13, equal-season +5.75,
with about 0.083 seconds per selection. This admitted the new integration stage;
it did not establish adaptive transfer-policy gain. The legacy and chip-aware
expected selectors have equal realised totals on all twelve overlays, so a
separate chip-aware component gain is not established. All overlay outcomes,
including losses on H1/Solio rosters, remain in the aggregate.
The fixed 25% common-uniform appearance stress preserves marginals: the four
H6 paths average +4.25 in summed independent objective and +4.34 in stressed
objective versus saved selections. These are expected-objective diagnostics,
not additional realised gains or an adaptive correlated-policy benchmark.

## Meaning, costs and limitations

The appearance replay reproduces all 316,570 original point means exactly across
76 origins; there is no new model fit or point-error improvement by construction.
The 1% probability mixture is a fixed decomposition assumption, not a calibration
improvement: raw Brier is slightly lower on both all-listed and saved-owned cohorts.
Expected returns conditional on appearance are reconstructed, not newly learned.
Realised scoring retains zero-minute cards even though the primary forecast
omits that rare event; neither saved-owned cohort contains one.

Two source-preparation failures are retained: the first lacked its source-checkout
import path before loading data; the second failed mean reproduction because a
derived historical correction omitted appearance labels. A regression-tested
repair reran all origins uniformly; no fitted weights, forecasts or probability
parameters were selected from those failures.

The new policy evaluates expected legal autosubs/vice fallback, counts Bench Boost
players once and handles Triple Captain fallback. Its inner MILPs still propose
under the old mean proxy; expected-value reranking is a bounded heuristic, not
an exact global expected-transfer/chip optimisation. Expected-objective and global
bounds stay unknown. Actual source/rules/news/price updates are used at each
origin; future prices, recoveries and results cannot inform the earlier action.

Separate implementations reconcile 608 settlements,
2708 retained provisional periods,
852 expected-objective periods and
5676 completed native proposal periods. Candidate trial bound
semantics and remaining-chip reserve are audited. Independent means separately
implemented checks within this project, not third-party certification.

The first batch retained H1 2256, H6 2383 and changed H6 2443, then failed strict
native admission. These incomplete results are not selected as the primary study.
The pinned native model allowed excess paid-transfer slack in a time-limit
incumbent; explicit canonicalisation preserves its roster/cash/free transfers and
retains every original reply. All sixteen cases reran uniformly after the tested
amendment. The removed 424 units in that diagnostic were provisional objective
penalty, **not 424 realised FPL points**. Legacy admission remains strict by default.

The final sixteen paths took 84.45 serial minutes,
with maximum sampled process-group memory 964.2 MiB,
two threads and no GPU/cloud or new dependency. Failed/superseded attempts and
separate preparation/compatibility costs remain disclosed. These are ordinary-load
historical replay timings; audits and lightweight synthetic checks overlapped
parts of the batch. Runtime versions differ from repository pins and are retained
in the aggregate. A completed historical batch is not the time required for one
weekly forecast or planning decision.

An explicit optional [approved-artifact bridge](season_decision_value.md#optional-approved-artifact-compatibility)
preserves approved weights, observation/source clocks, mean forecasts and
current-only manual sensitivities. Invented-input compatibility, even with the
actual approved artifact, is not live accuracy evidence. No registry or normal
weekly-default change is made by this release.

## Next research decisions

The aggregate includes per-case base-XI, autosub, armband, bench-boost and hit
accounting, plus changed roster/transfer/chip deadlines. These independent policy
paths diverge; component differences are not causal recoverable gains or evidence
that one component alone produced the season total.
Fresh controls can differ materially from earlier released paths under wall-time-
bounded search. All such repeat differences are disclosed; they are not a
calibrated noise distribution, and no earlier or best control replaces the fresh
predeclared comparison. This sensitivity further limits a causal superiority claim.

In the losing 2025/26 case, the changed path has +61 autosub points but −112
base-XI points and saves 16 hit points, yielding −33 overall after the other
components. All four paths have greater autosub contributions, but their rosters
and chip dates diverge. This accounting supports investigating whole-roster
return/availability coherence and search stability; it does not identify an
isolated recoverable gain or justify fitting to that losing case.

Before any production-policy proposal, predeclare a repeatability study using
fixed work limits or matched repetitions, retaining both cost and all outcomes.
Fresh H6 controls differ from the earlier release by 0, −110, −61 and 0 points;
this is material next to the observed policy effect. The explicit native repair
also means external repeat differences are not a pure noise sample. No additional
settings or repeats were chosen here to improve the published result.

Use the measured whole-season result when deciding whether to extend the policy;
do not infer a transfer gain from the earlier +5.75 lineup overlay. Inspect
appearance/conditional-return coherence and horizon-edge resource value before
larger learners or more solver time. Previously unsuccessful conditional-return,
expected-selector and preseason studies remain controls and do not justify an
unchanged retry. Additional years require admitted deadline markets/calendars,
prior-only forecasts, complete outcomes and their actual financial/chip rules.
Future frozen weekly review remains maintenance, never a wait required to finish
this historical implementation.
