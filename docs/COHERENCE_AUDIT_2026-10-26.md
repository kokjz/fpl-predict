Role unions count each player/forecast cell once per role. Different roles overlap;
per-case role metrics and all fixed bins/longer leads remain in the
[full aggregate](coherence_audit_results_2026-10-26.json). Conditional errors use
only observed appearances with positive predicted probability; this is an
outcome-conditioned diagnostic, never a player-eligibility rule for planning.

Appearance probabilities are lower than observed appearance rates in both
current-origin proposed-squad unions, by approximately 5.0 and 2.8 percentage
points. Regularising the original fixture marginals does not improve the Brier
score in these current-origin unions. Brier also measures discrimination and
outcome uncertainty, so the reliability bins/counts remain necessary.

No implied conditional return exceeds 20 points across the complete declared
forecast grid; maxima are about 15.1 and 13.7 in the two years. The proposed-squad
current-origin conditional biases are small relative to their conditional RMSE.
This does not establish complete conditional calibration, but does not support
extreme `mean / p_play` inflation as the leading diagnosis on this evidence.

The full market contains 26 and 23 observed appearances despite a frozen zero
probability; one newer-year proposed-squad union observation has this property.
These can include official-zero/source assumptions. They are not authority to
clear fitness flags or manually replace probabilities. Card-only outcomes and
missing-outcome counts are separately reported.

Joint squad-count residuals differ across years and cases. Marginal calibration
errors, correlated appearances and overlapping squad exposures are not isolated
by those descriptive diagnostics. No causal dependence estimate, significance
claim or independent-season confidence interval is inferred.

The measured stages took roughly 20 seconds to freeze and 4 seconds to evaluate,
with maximum sampled process-group memory below 530 MiB. A failed launch before
calculation is retained separately: the private launcher omitted the source-path
setting, then reran the same protocol/calculation with that environment fixed.
No model was fitted or new data downloaded for this diagnostic.
