## Forecasting studies and interpretation
Official injury percentages are retained as observations, not assigned start probabilities. A [controlled availability study](INCUMBENT_AVAILABILITY_RESULTS_2026-10-16.md) reduced candidate appearance Brier error by 20.6% against the full incumbent method. A subsequent [conditional-return and selection study](CONDITIONAL_SELECTION_RESULTS_2026-10-16.md) explicitly integrated those probabilities: its research challenger improved audit points RMSE by 1.23% and MAE by 0.73% versus the incumbent reference, but had lower ranking quality. The new expected bench-selection policy did not establish a realised points gain, so **v1.0.0 remains approved**. These are reused historical error measurements, not percentages of FPL points gained. The earlier [text experiment](NEWS_AVAILABILITY_RESULTS_2026-10-16.md) found no extra Brier benefit from combining news text with structured labels.

The [historical market-strength study](MARKET_STRENGTH_STAGE_C_RESULTS_2026-10-28.md)
passes its team-goal component test but fails the required player-development
RMSE gains: pooled player-GW and fixture improvements are 0.417% and 0.402%,
below 0.5%. Its fixed-squad mean is +10.175 points, with individual deltas
−36 to +54; that does not override the failed error gates. **Approved 1.0.0 and
weekly defaults remain unchanged.** No later-season scoring or season paths.

The [expected-return training-target study](EXPECTED_RETURN_TARGETS_RESULTS_2026-10-29.md)
passes admission but fails development: pooled candidate RMSE improves only
0.165%/0.146%, while MAE worsens 0.598%/0.571%, outside the fixed gates.
Its positive fixed-squad mean does not override the failures. **Approved 1.0.0
and weekly defaults remain unchanged.**

The [rich-history and market-strength combination study](RICH_MARKET_COMBINATION_RESULTS_2026-11-02.md)
passes development; its pre-registered 2024/25 confirmation passes. The combination
was selected adaptively on reused folds; confirmation outcomes had appeared in
earlier policy studies. **Approved 1.0.0 and weekly defaults remain unchanged.**

The [promotion-evidence follow-up](RMC_PROMOTION_EVIDENCE_RESULTS_2026-11-02.md)
stops at Understat's source policy before data retrieval or fitting; compliant
live inputs are unavailable for the combination in this form. Nothing promoted.

The [FPL-native and market variant](FPL_NATIVE_COMBINATION_RESULTS_2026-11-02.md) proves Understat-free feature construction but misses its 0.5% development RMSE gates (0.422% player-GW and 0.414% fixture gains). Conditional audits were not run. **Approved 1.0.0 and weekly defaults remain unchanged.**

The [capacity-matched compliant combination](FPL_NATIVE_CAPACITY_RESULTS_2026-11-02.md) misses its development gates (RMSE gains 0.460% player-GW and 0.454% fixture; NDCG also below control). Stage C was not run. **Approved 1.0.0 and weekly defaults remain unchanged.**

The [component-Poisson study](COMPONENT_POISSON_RESULTS_2026-11-03.md) fails development gates (7/10): candidate RMSE worsens by 0.047% per player-GW and 0.061% per fixture, with lower NDCG. Stage C was not triggered. **Approved 1.0.0 and weekly defaults remain unchanged.**

The latest [forecaster-only qualification](FORECASTER_QUALIFICATION_RESULTS_2026-10-16.md) tests adoption independently of the expected-bench
selector. Older-season development is nearly neutral (0.0056% RMSE gain), below
the existing qualification threshold. The candidate retains its 2025 RMSE gain
and small owned-squad gain, but scores fewer points in reset and rolling-transfer
comparisons. It is saved as a research artifact; **v1.0.0 remains approved**.
