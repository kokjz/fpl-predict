## Design and limits

One combined challenger joins the 49 CORE features, 256 RICH features and three MARKET features in that order, with stable deduplication:308 before training-only all-NaN omissions. Both methods use the same seven-leaf HGB,200 iterations, learning rate0.05,L2=20,min leaf60,no early stopping,seed20260921,730.5-day age weights,two threads and fixture clip[0,20]. Actual-point targets, candidate predicate, cutoffs, DGW aggregation and evaluation are shared. Rich prefix histories are strictly before deadline minus3h; market ratings use only matches kicking off before that cutoff.

The combination was chosen adaptively after observing near-misses on reused2022/23–2023/24 folds. Its development fixture margin was thin:2.976947456702168 vs the2.9790953648626277 threshold. Therefore a development pass alone was insufficient. The2024/25 confirmation was pre-registered before outcomes, separately authorised, trained2020/21–2023/24 and tested2024/25. Those outcomes had been seen in earlier season-policy studies with the approved forecaster; they never selected this method.
