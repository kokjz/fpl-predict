## Read-only initial review

`review` validates primary designation and frozen trial hashes, requires exactly
one scored outcome file, and independently recomputes points from its retained
finished/checked raw source. Tampered summaries or changed source bytes fail;
missing/ambiguous outcome files stay pending. No input is modified. Review output
is aggregate-only, write-once, with evidence hashes and no individual squad data.

Group separately by season,model version/hash and chosen baseline. Unlabelled,
unfinished or unscored trials cannot inflate the sample. Each series needs at
least **eight contiguous observed GWs** for the initial descriptive four-GW-block
interval; gaps stay missing rather than zero. Eight weeks is a review point, not
proof of superiority, a stopping guarantee or an automatic promotion. Continue
if evidence is inconclusive. No across-version/baseline winner is selected.

Use normal `score` forecast-error/minutes/appearance/official-baseline reports
alongside the decision-point review. The latter evaluates legal weekly lineup,
captain,autosub and vice fallback on the same observed squad, without transfer
costs,chips or alternative squad finances. Across-week roster changes are observed
manager choices, not a simulated model transfer policy. Minutes overrides make
the comparison one of the documented model-assisted workflow, not necessarily
the statistical engine alone. Do not infer net season-strategy gains from it.

A future challenger still needs the unchanged development,error,point-relevance,
source integrity, contemporary-scoring and operational-cost gates. Freeze its
portable artifact/schema/approved-for-shadow status before any prospective use;
never install a benchmark winner merely because its code merged. With no current
