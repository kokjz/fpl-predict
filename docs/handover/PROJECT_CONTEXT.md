and adaptive season-management research engine. The eventual target is maximum
expected net38GWpoints under real changing information and legal finances/chips.

engine and benchmarks methods. No account automation is implemented/authorised.

with mixed MAE and noisy candidate-pool minutes errors. These were forecast-error
results, not reliable FPL points gains. Subsequent small/ensemble challengers did
