diagnostics, not prospective or actual-manager season gains. Positive deltas do

## Decision

Stop this study with no finalist. Do not run extra seeds, tune blend weights or
score later audits for these rejected methods. Historical source-time/statistical
correction and price/schedule limitations remain; previous windows remain reused.
The research pipeline is inexpensive, but greater complexity is not justified by
this study. Proceed with bounded source admission and defensible external-source
qualification; preserve prospective evaluation requirements and approved weights.
